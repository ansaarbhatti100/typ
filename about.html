COMPANY PROFILE

ESTABLISHED SINCE 2016

WHO WE ARE

Bays Sports is a premier manufacturer and exporter of custom sportswear and sports goods based in the global manufacturing hub of Sialkot, Pakistan.

Since its inception in 2016, the company has established itself as a trusted partner for international B2B clients, white-label brands, and Amazon FBA sellers. We specialize in high-performance team uniforms, lifestyle activewear, and specialized sports gear.

Starting as a small-scale producer of team uniforms, Bays Sports has rapidly expanded its product line to include complex athletic gear, fitness wear, and specialized accessories like custom bags and gloves.

OUR MISSION

To deliver superior-quality, high-performance sports apparel at competitive prices while ensuring maximum customer satisfaction through innovation, precision craftsmanship, and reliable service.

OUR VISION

To become a world-renowned leader in the custom sportswear industry, recognized for setting high standards in quality, durability, and client-centric solutions for athletes everywhere.
