GENERAL POLICIES

Our commitment to transparency and ethical business practices.

Order & Payment

We require a 50% deposit to initiate production for custom bulk orders. The remaining 50% is due upon completion and before shipment. We accept Bank Transfers, PayPal, and major Credit Cards.

Returns & Quality

Since all products are custom-manufactured, we do not offer returns for change of mind. However, we guarantee quality; if a product has a manufacturing defect, we will replace it free of charge.

Ethical Manufacturing

Bays Sports is committed to fair labor practices. No child labor is used in any stage of our production, and we maintain a safe, clean, and healthy working environment for all our staff.
